# Design Psychology & Conversion Furniture

This file is the menu for Phase 3 and Phase 8. Pick 4-6 items max per build. Furniture stacked everywhere reads as desperate, not premium.

## The Three Core Premium Principles

1. **The Halo Effect (50ms):** First impression colors everything after. Engineer the hero. Bespoke graphics > stock.
2. **Cognitive Fluency:** Brains are lazy. Simple = trustworthy. Whitespace is the primary directive. One goal per section.
3. **Peak-End Rule:** People remember peaks and endings. Micro-interactions (hover feedback, form confirmations, smooth arrivals) create the peaks.

## The UX Psychology Menu

| Principle | Furniture | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| **Smart Defaults** | Pre-fill forms with most common choices. Button states show remaining slots. | Forms, booking flows |
| **Goal Gradient** | Progress starts at 20%. First step already checked. | Multi-step onboarding, checkout |
| **Reciprocity** | Give a real artifact first (mini audit, price estimate, checklist). | Mid-page, before the ask |
| **IKEA Effect** | Let them build/choose something before signup (name, palette, card style). | Pre-signup flows |
| **Loss Aversion** | Name what inaction costs (missed appointments, empty chairs). Honest only. No fake countdowns. | Pricing, proof sections |
| **Contrast Effect** | Show premium tier first so the retainer reads small. | Pricing order |
| **Status Quo Bias** | "I'll risk it" instead of "Maybe later" on dismiss buttons. | Upgrade prompts |

## Bespoke Assets & Brand Guidelines

- **Custom Graphics:** Must match brand tone and aesthetic. If it looks out of place, it's worse than no graphic.
- **Cohesion:** Palette must work together to create a mood, not just 5 colors that look good alone.
- **Typography:** Childish, outdated, or hard-to-read fonts instantly cheapen the site. Right fonts make it feel more sophisticated than the brand actually is.

## Subtle Animation (The Anti-Rage-Click Layer)

- Fade-ins on scroll, hover responses, natural section arrivals.
- **Goal:** Guide attention smoothly. Prevent overwhelm.
- **Rule:** Subtle enough to not be consciously noticed, but felt.

## Structure & Ownership

- **Sitemap:** Every page leads somewhere. Clear journey to CTA.
- **SEO/AIO:** Structured for algorithms AND humans.
- **Ownership:** Client must be able to update it. A site that feels abandoned feels cheap. Freshness = aliveness = premium.

## The "Premium" Checklist (For Client Pitches)

1. Engineered first impression (Halo).
2. Zero cognitive load (Fluency).
3. Moments of delight (Peak-End).
4. Bespoke, cohesive assets.
5. Clear structure and CTA.
6. Client ownership and freshness.
