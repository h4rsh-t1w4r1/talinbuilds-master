# Master Prompt & Thinking Framework

This file is the front door to every build. No design decision exists until it is written here and approved. It prevents AI slop by forcing explicit choices before generation begins.

## Part A: The Thinking Framework (Eight Decisions)

Each decision lists its output and its slop trap. The trap is the part that saves you.

### D1. Category and Trust Mode
**Output:** Dark or light, and the anxiety level of the buyer.
- Clinics, education, government: light, clarity over drama.
- Anxiety-heavy buyers get more polish budget (polish = competence).
**Slop Trap:** Defaulting everything to near-black cinematic because it photographs well in portfolios.

### D2. One Pain, One Outcome (Buyer's Words)
**Output:** Two quoted phrases from real reviews/forums, plus the single promise the site makes.
- If the subject is a person (owner, chef, doctor), the hero speaks in their confident voice. Pain language lives below the fold.
**Slop Trap:** Opening with someone else's complaint over the owner's face.

### D3. One CTA and Funnel Order
**Output:** The CTA verb plus the ordered list of sections that earn the next scroll toward it.
**Slop Trap:** Three competing CTAs in the hero and a footer burying the real one.

### D4. Visual World (60-30-10 System)
**Output:** 
- Primary 60% (backgrounds/surfaces, real-world reference)
- Secondary 30% (cards, nav, dividers)
- Accent 10% (CTAs, active states, 1-2 emphasis moments, nowhere else)
- Canvas is never pure black or pure white. Palette sampled from the subject's material world.
**Slop Trap:** Five colors that fight each other, or an accent sprayed everywhere.

### D5. Type Trio
**Output:** 
- Display face (real character, NEVER Inter/Roboto)
- Quiet body face
- Mono for small labels
- Exact weights, H1 min size, body line height 1.6-1.8, measure 65-75 chars.
**Slop Trap:** One family at three weights pretending to be a system.

### D6. Motion Mode & Five-Part Hero
**Output:** Mode S, C, or W. Hero spec: outcome headline, subheadline (specific detail + timeframe), single CTA (no competitor), labeled visual slot, one trust signal.
**Slop Trap:** "City's Premier X" headlines and a hero with two buttons.

### D7. Conversion Furniture (from Psychology Menu)
**Output:** A placement list, each item naming the principle it serves. Pick 4-6 items max.
**Slop Trap:** Furniture stacked everywhere.

### D8. Signature and Bans
**Output:** One signature element (test: remove it, does the page change?), plus the ban list (AI clichés, no adjacent sections sharing layout skeleton, no lorem, no em dashes, no stock words).
**Slop Trap:** A signature that is just a decorative icon.
